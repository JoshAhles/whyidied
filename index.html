INTEL / FAQ
What is WhyIDied?
WhyIDied is a Fortnite AI coach for Windows that automatically records the last ~60 seconds before every death, uses AI to explain the exact mistake that caused it, and tracks which mistakes you keep repeating so you can actually fix them. No clip uploads, no VOD scrubbing — it watches so you don't have to.
Why do I keep dying in Fortnite?
Almost always: the same 2–3 recurring mistakes — unprotected peeks, late rotations, fights taken without mats or high ground. You don't notice the pattern because nobody reviews their deaths. WhyIDied reviews all of them and shows you the recurring cause with video evidence and a timestamp. Full breakdown: the seven mistakes that cause most deaths.
Is this a cheat? Will it get me banned?
No. WhyIDied never touches the Fortnite process: no injection, no memory reading, no overlays, nothing live during a match. It records your own screen using the same Windows capture system streamers use, and reads the local replay files Fortnite already saves on your PC. Everything happens after the match.
How does it actually know why I died?
Two sources, combined: video of your final 60 seconds, and structured match data from Fortnite's own replay file (positions, storm, damage, materials). A multimodal AI reviews both, finds the causal mistake — not just the last five seconds — and cites the timestamp. Every diagnosis feeds your weakness history, so patterns surface across games.
Will it hurt my FPS?
Capture runs on your GPU's hardware encoder — the same path Shadowplay uses — and analysis runs after you die, never during a fight. Performance is a launch requirement: we'll publish benchmarks, and if the numbers aren't clean we don't ship.
What will it cost?
A genuinely useful free tier at launch. Pro is planned at $14.99/month — and the first 100 waitlist founders lock $9.99/month for as long as they stay subscribed.
What happens to my recordings?
Clips stay on your PC by default; only short evidence around analyzed deaths is uploaded, encrypted, with retention limits. Delete any clip or your whole account anytime. Mic capture is off by default, and your footage is never used to train AI models without separate, explicit consent. WhyIDied is for players 13+.
