01 · Method

Every death, reviewed. Not the ones you remember to clip.

You already know you died. What you do not know is which of the last twelve seconds decided it — the peek, the rotation, the wall you never replaced — and whether you have done the same thing forty times this month.

01

It records

The last sixty seconds before every death, automatically, using the same hardware encoder as an OBS replay buffer. Nothing to clip, nothing to scrub, nothing running during the fight.

02

It reads

Every number it reasons with is measured, from your own screen and the match's own record — never estimated, never inferred from your rank. And when a number is not there to be measured, it does not invent one.

03

It rules

A cause, the contributing factors, and the second each one happened at. You can rule against any finding, and that goes on the record — a coach confident enough to be marked wrong is worth more than one that is never tested.

03 · The refusal

When it cannot see, it says so.

Any tool can always have an answer. That is not a feature — it is the absence of one. When a channel has nothing to report, this one prints NO READING and the reason beside it. Never a zero, never a flat line, never quietly hidden.

A coach who is never unsure is a coach you cannot trust when they are certain.

06 · Questions

Straight answers

Is this a cheat? Will it get me banned?

No. It never touches the Fortnite process — no injection, no memory reading, no overlay, and nothing at all running during a match. It records your own screen with the same Windows capture system OBS uses, and reads the replay files Fortnite already saves on your PC. Every bit of coaching happens after the match is over.

Will it cost me frames?

Recording runs on your GPU's hardware encoder, the same path Shadowplay and OBS replay buffers use, and analysis waits until you are dead. We capture video, so the burden of proof is ours — we will publish the benchmark rather than assert a number.

How does it know why I died?

Two sources. Video of your final sixty seconds, and the structured match data from the replay file — positions, storm, damage, materials. The numbers are read off the frame by measurement rather than guessed at, and the model rules on the causal mistake rather than describing the last shot. If a reading is not there, it says so instead of inventing one.

What does it cost?

Your first three deaths are investigated free, once — enough to see exactly what you get. Pro is planned at $14.99/month, and the first 100 waitlist founders lock $9.99/month for as long as they stay subscribed.

What happens to my recordings?

Clips stay on your PC by default. Only the short evidence around an analysed death is uploaded, over an encrypted connection, and you can delete any clip or your whole account whenever you like. Microphone capture is off by default. We never train models on your footage without separate, explicit consent. For players 13 and up.
